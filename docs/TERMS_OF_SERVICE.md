## 2. Definitions

- **Handshake (HNS):** A decentralized, permissionless naming protocol for top-level domains.
- **TLD:** Top-Level Domain registered on the Handshake blockchain (e.g., `.cryptofans`, `.halcyon-domains`).
- **Subdomain:** A second-level domain registered under an HSR-managed TLD (e.g., `yourname.cryptofans`).
- **FQDN:** Fully Qualified Domain Name — the complete subdomain including the TLD.
- **Subdomain NFT:** An ERC-721 non-fungible token minted on the Flare Network representing ownership rights to a registered subdomain.
- **FLR:** The native token of the Flare Network, used for all transactions on the platform.
- **Wallet:** A blockchain-compatible cryptocurrency wallet (e.g., MetaMask) used to authenticate and transact on the Service.
- **Registration Period:** The active duration of a subdomain registration, currently one (1) year from date of purchase.
- **Grace Period:** A sixty (60) day window following expiration during which a subdomain may be renewed before reclamation.
- **Marketplace:** The secondary trading platform for Subdomain NFTs, supporting fixed-price listings, offers, and auctions.

## 4. NFT Ownership Rights and Limitations

### 4.1 What You Own

When you purchase a Subdomain NFT, you receive:

- **An ERC-721 token** on the Flare Network representing your registration rights to the specified subdomain for the Registration Period.
- **DNS resolution rights** — the subdomain will resolve via Handshake DNS for the duration of your registration.
- **The right to transfer or sell** your Subdomain NFT on the HSR Marketplace or any compatible NFT marketplace, subject to on-chain royalty enforcement.
- **The right to renew** your subdomain registration prior to the expiration of the Grace Period.
- **The right to customize** your NFT artwork through the platform's AI art generation system, subject to available regeneration credits.

### 4.2 What You Do Not Own

Ownership of a Subdomain NFT does **not** grant you:

- **Ownership of the parent TLD.** All Handshake TLDs remain the exclusive property of HSR. You have no claim, right, or interest in any TLD itself.
- **Permanent or perpetual rights.** Subdomain registrations expire after the Registration Period and must be renewed. Failure to renew within the Grace Period results in forfeiture.
- **Intellectual property rights to HSR.** You receive no rights to HSR's trademarks, branding, platform code, AI models, or proprietary systems.
- **Intellectual property rights to AI-generated base artwork.** The AI-generated artwork associated with your NFT is provided as a visual representation of your subdomain. HSR retains the right to use similar artistic styles and themes across the platform.
- **Hosting or infrastructure services.** HSR provides DNS resolution only. We do not provide web hosting, email hosting, SSL/TLS certificates, content delivery, or any server infrastructure.
- **Guaranteed secondary market value.** HSR makes no representations regarding the present or future monetary value of any Subdomain NFT.

### 4.3 NFT Transferability

- Subdomain NFTs may be freely transferred between Flare Network wallets.
- All secondary sales are subject to a **2.5% creator royalty** (ERC-2981) and a **2.5% platform fee** when transacted through the HSR Marketplace.
- Royalties are enforced on-chain and cannot be waived.
- Transfer of the NFT transfers all associated subdomain rights, including DNS resolution and renewal rights, to the new holder.
- Upon transfer, the previous holder immediately loses all rights to the subdomain.

### 4.4 Expiration and Reclamation

- Subdomain registrations are valid for one (1) year from the date of purchase or renewal.
- A sixty (60) day Grace Period follows expiration, during which you may renew at the standard renewal price.
- After the Grace Period, the subdomain is reclaimed by HSR and the associated NFT is rendered non-functional. DNS resolution ceases.
- HSR reserves the right to re-register reclaimed subdomains to new users.

### 6.1 Pricing

- Subdomain prices are denominated in USD and payable in FLR tokens on the Flare Network.
- FLR/USD conversion rates are determined by the Flare Time Series Oracle (FTSO) at the time of transaction.
- Pricing varies by subdomain character length and TLD tier. Current pricing is displayed on the platform prior to purchase.
- HSR reserves the right to modify pricing at any time. Price changes do not affect existing registrations or pending renewals.

### 6.2 Payment

- All transactions are executed on-chain via smart contracts on the Flare Network.
- Payment is made in FLR tokens. No fiat currency, credit cards, or other cryptocurrencies are accepted at this time.
- You are responsible for maintaining sufficient FLR balance and paying all associated network gas fees.
- Transactions are processed through the SubdomainEscrow smart contract. FLR is held in escrow until DNS verification is completed by the DNSOracle.

### 6.3 Refund Policy

**Due to the nature of blockchain transactions and NFT minting, all sales are final.**

- **Completed registrations** — No refunds are issued for successfully registered subdomains. Once DNS verification is complete and the NFT is minted, the transaction is irreversible.
- **Failed registrations** — If DNS verification fails and the NFT is not minted, escrowed FLR is automatically returned to your wallet via the smart contract. No manual refund request is necessary.
- **Renewal payments** — Renewal payments are non-refundable once processed.
- **Marketplace transactions** — Secondary market purchases and sales are final. Platform fees and royalties are non-refundable.
- **AI art regeneration credits** — Regeneration credits are non-refundable once purchased.
- **Network fees** — Blockchain gas fees are never refundable, regardless of transaction outcome.

### 6.4 Chargebacks and Disputes

As all transactions occur on-chain, traditional chargeback mechanisms do not apply. If you believe a transaction was executed in error due to a platform malfunction (not user error or market conditions), you may submit a dispute as outlined in Section 7.
